## Update — initial wait raised to 90s

The runs above executed with a 45s initial wait. That budget was later raised
to **90s** across US-001/US-002/US-003 after a 1m15s run was observed on a
just-booted simulator with a freshly installed app. Note the 45s value was not
reproducibly failing (follow-up attempts passed in 27s and 18s), so the larger
budget is a safety margin for cold environments rather than a fix for a known
failure; `extendedWaitUntil` returns as soon as the element appears, so it
costs nothing on warm launches.
