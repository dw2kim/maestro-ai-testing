- **Run:** `runs/20260731-135133` (representative pass)

- **Device:** iPhone 16 Pro — iOS 18.6 simulator (FEB45C9A)

- **Result:** PASS (stable after fix — see Fix and verification)

## Summary

US-002 verifies navigation: from a cold, cleared launch the flow lands on
Home, taps the "Journey" bottom-nav tab, and asserts the Journey screen
(title, Workout summary card, Total Average metric). The flow passes in
~14–16s. During stabilization it flaked at the initial "wait for Home" step
on slow cold launches; raising that wait to 45s made it stable (details
below). No app defects found.

## Failure analysis

**Observed intermittently: timeout at "wait for Home".** At the 15s timeout
(original flow) the screen contained only the app-level `"One Punch"`
container and status-bar chrome — Home content (day list, nav) had not
rendered. Screenshotting the same device ~20s later showed Home fully
rendered, so the app was **slow, not hung**.

- **Expected vs actual:** expected Home to render within the 15s wait;
  actual cold-launch render time varied and sometimes exceeded 15s.
- **Root cause:** slow cold `clearState` launch. The launch re-seeds data
  and warms the Flutter engine; render time is variable (observed ~5s to
  >20s), and the flow's initial wait was too short to absorb the slow tail.
  An earlier hypothesis — "relaunching while foregrounded causes it" — was
  **disproven**: terminating the app first did not prevent a later failure.
- **Confidence:** high. Verified by direct observation (Home rendered after
  the wait expired) and by fix verification (below).
- **Classification:** **test issue, not an app bug.** The app cold-starts
  correctly; the flow simply needed to wait longer.

## Fix and verification

Raised the initial `extendedWaitUntil` timeout from 15s to **45s**
(`extendedWaitUntil` returns as soon as the element appears, so a large
timeout is free on fast launches and only spends time on slow ones).

| Condition | Before (15s wait) | After (45s wait) |
|---|---|---|
| App foregrounded, then run | FAIL | 3/3 PASS (14–15s) |
| Clean state, then run | 4/4 PASS | (unchanged) |

`run_flow.sh` also now terminates the target app before each run for a clean
starting state — good hygiene, but note it is **not** what fixed this
failure; the generous wait is.

## Recommendations

Ordered by priority:

1. **Done — generous initial wait.** Flows should wait on a screen-specific
   element with a 45s timeout right after a `clearState` launch. Applied to
   US-001 and US-002; captured in `prompts/generate-flow.md` and `CLAUDE.md`.
2. **No app bugs found.** Journey navigation and rendering are correct on a
   clean cold start.
3. **New grounding rule captured:** on this Flutter app a card subtree can
   collapse into a single accessibility label
   (`"Workout\nTotal Average: 00:00\nM\nT\nW\nT\nF\nS\nS"`); assert substrings
   of it with a DOTALL regex — inner texts are not separately targetable.
   Recorded in `prompts/generate-flow.md` and `CLAUDE.md`.
