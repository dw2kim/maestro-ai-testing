## Summary

US-002 verifies navigation: from a cold, cleared launch the flow lands on
Home, taps the "Journey" bottom-nav tab, and asserts the Journey screen
(title, Workout summary card, Total Average metric). On a clean device state
the flow passes in ~15–16s. One of five runs failed at the initial
"wait for Home" step because the app was relaunched while still foregrounded
from a prior session (see Reliability).

## Failure analysis

**Observed once (1/5): timeout at "wait for Home".** At the 15s timeout the
screen contained only the app-level `"One Punch"` container and status-bar
chrome — Home content (day list, nav) had not rendered.

- **Expected vs actual:** expected Home to render within 15s of a cleared
  launch; actual was a blank splash that never resolved during the wait.
- **Most likely cause:** flaky timing on cold launch. The failing run
  relaunched the app while it was still foregrounded (from manual
  exploration on the Journey tab); the clean runs terminated the app first.
- **Confidence:** high. Reproduction rate dropped to 0/4 once the app was
  terminated before each run.
- **Classification:** **test/environment issue, not an app bug.** No evidence
  the app misbehaves on a normal cold start.

## Recommendations

Ordered by priority:

1. **Make the run harness start from a clean app state** (fix the test
   process): terminate the target app before `maestro test`, or have the flow
   tolerate a slow first frame. Low-cost, removes the only observed failure.
   Candidate: add a pre-launch `xcrun simctl terminate` step (iOS) to
   `run_flow.sh`, or bump the initial `extendedWaitUntil` timeout.
2. **No app bugs found.** Journey navigation and rendering are correct on a
   clean cold start.
3. **New grounding rule captured:** on this Flutter app a card subtree can
   collapse into a single accessibility label
   (`"Workout\nTotal Average: 00:00\nM\nT\nW\nT\nF\nS\nS"`); assert substrings
   of it with a DOTALL regex — inner texts are not separately targetable.
   Recorded in `prompts/generate-flow.md` and `CLAUDE.md`.
